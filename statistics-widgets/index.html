Introduction

  • The Binomial distribution Binomial(k | n, p) as a function of k, with sliders for n and p.
  • The Binomial likelihood The same expression as a function of p, with the data k and n held fixed.
  • Distribution or likelihood? One Binomial surface, cut two ways: a distribution over x that sums to 1, and a likelihood for p that does not.
  • The Beta distribution Beta(p | α, β) as a function of p — a distribution over a probability.
  • A mixture of two Normals Two Normal densities added together with weights that sum to 1 — and a mean that can land in the valley between them.
  • The prior predictive A Beta prior and a Binomial likelihood, averaged into the counts the model expects before seeing data.
  • From prior to posterior Prior × likelihood, pointwise, and the Beta posterior that comes out of it.
