Rhomb Decagon

Tile a regular decagon of integer side length n with unit Penrose rhombs. Placement is free-form: rotate a rhomb, move it anywhere inside the boundary, and snap to existing vertices when useful.

Tile Type
Rotation
0°
Board Rotation

Controls

  • Move the mouse or touch inside the board to position the current rhomb.
  • Click or tap to place when the preview is highlighted as valid.
  • Click any placed rhomb to delete it.
  • Rotate in 36° steps with Q and E.
  • Cycle through all 8 tile types with T.
  • Snapping uses decagon unit points and vertices of already placed rhombs, but free placement is still allowed.
