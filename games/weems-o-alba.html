On a keyboard

WASD or arrows tae move. Hold Space, J or the left mouse button tae strike. K, Shift or right-click for yer special. H eats a haggis. P pauses.

On a phone

Put yer thumb doon anywhere on the movement side and drag tae move. Hold the red button tae strike, tap the blue one for yer special.

Wee glossary

Hale is yer health. Smeddum is yer fighting spirit, spent on specials. Merks are auld Scots coin, spent at the brownie's shop. He waits near the start o' every floor once ye're past the first boss. A kist is a treasure chest. Find the stair tae go deeper. On yer map, gold marks the stair, a pale blue bar a Pictish stane, and a green cross the brownie.
