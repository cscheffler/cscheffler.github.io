    Experiments

    1. Box too low. Choose Beta. Set the top of the box to 1. Make 5,000 points. Where is the histogram wrong? Why?
    2. Box too high. Choose Normal. Set the top of the box to 1. What happens to the acceptance rate? Are the samples still correct?
    3. No normalizing constant. Choose Trig curve. The area under this curve is not 1. Does the histogram still have the shape of the curve?
    4. Same seed, same points. Make 5 points and write down x₁. Press Reset and make 5 points again. Is x₁ the same?
