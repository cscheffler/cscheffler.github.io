Printable grid

Print this, colour it in, photograph it, and bring it back into the editor with Paper to pixel. One square on the page is one pixel on the card.

  1. Print at 100% — no "fit to page", or the squares stop being square.
  2. Colour the squares. Staying inside the lines helps, but only the middle of each square is read, so it does not have to be neat.
  3. Leave a square blank for transparent. White crayon will not come back — on the display, and to the camera, white paper and nothing are the same thing.
  4. Photograph the whole sheet in even light. Avoid a hard shadow across the page; a slight angle is fine, that is what the corner handles are for.
