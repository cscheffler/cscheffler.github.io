Convolution by hand

A 6×6 greyscale image. 0 is black, 1 is white: a vertical bar four pixels tall.

The rule

Lay the kernel over a 3×3 patch of the image, multiply the nine pairs, add them up. That one number is the output for that position. Slide by one and repeat. With no padding, a 6×6 image and a 3×3 kernel give a 4×4 output.

out[i][j]  =  sum over the 3×3 patch starting at row i, column j, of  (image pixel × kernel weight)

Three questions

  1. What is the relationship between your two output grids?
  2. Take the largest number in each output grid. Are they the same?
  3. So: which of the two did the convolution give you — a feature map that moves with the object, or one that ignores where the object is? Which one does a classifier that must answer "is there a bar anywhere?" actually need, and what operation gets you from the first to the second?
